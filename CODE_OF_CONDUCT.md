# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be civil. Assume the other person is trying to be useful. Disagree about the
work, not about each other.

## What is expected

- Courtesy and patience, particularly towards people who are new to this.
- Criticism aimed at the code, the documentation or the idea.
- Accepting a decision you disagree with once it has been made, or forking,
  which is what the licence is for.

## What is not

- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of that kind.
- Encouraging any of the above.

This covers issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and anywhere
else the project is being represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are taken
seriously and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may block an account, with
or without notice, where this document has been breached. There is no appeals
process; this is a small project, not an institution.
