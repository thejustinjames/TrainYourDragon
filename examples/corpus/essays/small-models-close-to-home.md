The interesting property of a small model is not its size. It is where it runs.

## The trade you are making

A seven-billion-parameter model quantised to four bits will lose an argument
with anything served from a data centre. It reasons less well, it knows fewer
things, and it is more easily confused by a long document.

In exchange, nothing leaves the building. The draft of the thing you have not
published yet, the client note, the half-formed idea that would embarrass you
in a competitor's training corpus: all of it stays on a disk you own.

For a great many tasks that trade is obviously right, and for a few it is
obviously wrong. The mistake is making it once and never revisiting it.

## What runs well locally

Shaping, not thinking. Reformatting, not researching. Turning six bullet points
into four paragraphs that sound like you wrote them, so that the version you
edit is already in the right register.

Anything that requires the model to be correct about the world belongs
somewhere else, or belongs with a retrieval step attached, which is the same
admission in better clothes.
