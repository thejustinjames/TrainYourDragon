  Lantern is a retrieval tool for people who have to defend what they say. It
  indexes a set of documents, answers a question from them, and shows the

  - Answers carry the passage they came from
  - Runs against a local folder or a document store
  - Says "not in these documents" and means it
features:
  - title: Provenance by default

  - title: Refusal as a feature
    description: An empty result is a valid answer and is reported as one.

## Who it is for

Anyone who will be asked, later and in public, where a claim came from.

## Why it exists

Because a fluent answer with no source is worse than no answer. It costs the
same to read and it cannot be checked, so the checking never happens.
