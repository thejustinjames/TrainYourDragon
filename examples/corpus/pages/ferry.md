  Ferry is a small, dull piece of plumbing. It moves files between systems on a
  schedule, records every move, and refuses to overwrite anything it has not

status: Shipping
highlights:
  - Every move is logged and reversible
  - Refuses silent overwrites

## Who it is for

Teams whose integration is currently a person with a laptop and a habit.
